# V12: validate actual defensive spending against all current public routes

The goal remains a winning advantage against every distinct opponent. V11 failed
its selected preservation screen. This experiment starts from frozen default V10,
without V11's rejected immediate-capture rule, and tests a separate route-planning
error found in complete recorded episodes. Neither longer survival nor a valid
local action certificate establishes competitive superiority.

## Evidence and hypothesis

In the recorded V11 Juraj V3.5 case 12, the V5 planner at turn 863 credits 17 new
defense against a required 14. Its two-step owned transfer appears sufficient
under the additive estimate. Independent public-wire Dijkstra and the exact
instrumented frozen policy instead show the global enemy-to-home cost changing
26 → 20 → 19 after those static own transfers. Vacating the donor opens a cheaper
13-edge detour around the intended seven-edge geometric route. The actual turn-865
observation has cost 18 after intervening game events.

The turn-865 defender release is locally consistent: memory, arrival, target,
continuity and priority predicates pass, but the arrived target remains five
short of its current required defense. Forcing a permanent hold would hide the
failed plan certificate. An isolated build at 861 spends 45 but changes neither
the current global minimum (21) nor selected-target route cost (26); it is not
proven to have opened that bypass. Twenty original V11/V6 decisions, memories and
telemetry trees match in the predicate audit.

V12 tests whether checking the **actual issued action** against the full current
public cost field prevents these harmful transfers while retaining useful builds,
repositioning and successful attacks. It does not claim to certify an entire
future route. The motivating late observation comes from V11's changed trajectory;
new V12 games must start at turn zero against adaptive opponents, not start from
that favorable diagnostic state or use recorded future responses.

## Candidate contract

Wrap default V10 and inspect its final action after all defender, offensive,
pursuit and mobilization decisions. Under the same current-time growth and
projected-deathtouch convention before and after, calculate each currently visible
within-horizon enemy's positive army-minus-public-route-cost deficit. Project
actual own transfers, winning/losing ordinary combat and castle spending; a PASS
leaves all garrisons unchanged. Preserve an immediate legal enemy-general capture.

If the action increases any relevant deficit, search at most nine candidates:
the original V2 campaign action (including its build option), four highest-scoring
moves, and the best moves from four other sources. Order the eight move choices
by campaign score; skip duplicate effective transfers and choices no better than
the actual campaign PASS score. Check each actual replacement with the same
route test, stopping at the first accepted choice. If none passes, issue a
canonical PASS and record search exhaustion. This is a bounded search and may
miss a productive alternative. Record chosen rank, probes, rejection, exhaustion,
PASS and before/after maximum deficits; a maximum alone does not describe every
per-enemy comparison used by the guard.

On replacement, clear both unissued defender and offensive transport state while
retaining publicly observed stationary-general information and current timestep.
Inherited parent events are proposals when `route_guard_parent_action_issued` is
false. The disabled variant must reproduce V10 action, memory and telemetry exactly.
The prototype class is `generals.agents.sentinel_v12_agent.SentinelV12Agent`;
`guard_routes=False` selects the exact disabled control. V2 remains the default.

## Runtime, controls and limits

Use the original-version synchronous Amin adapter and its bound 18-history /
1,528-frame action-parity proof. Use original external stdio opponents with their
separate startup/response limits, plus synchronous official-version candidate
inference. Retain full public wires, original keys/actions/memory/telemetry,
terminal state, source identities and cleanup. This is strategy evidence without
a candidate deployment deadline claim.

Before games, require focused engine-backed action-projection tests, exact disabled
behavior, memory reset and real negative-control checks. The controls include a
productive rear build, the unchanged-bound build at 861, a justified screen move
into threatened home, and a captured defender that must release. Profile V10/V12
whole-step calls on identical official-version CPU fixtures, including active
replacement and retained-parent paths; separate cold compilation and require at
least half the parent's warm throughput. Record exhaustion and PASS behavior in
complete episodes rather than treating a safety veto as progress.

All coverage calculations describe current visible single-stack routes. They do
not predict simultaneous moves, merges, fog discoveries or a necessary temporary
weakening followed by a successful multi-turn maneuver. Longer weighted detours
and geometric arrival estimates are different quantities. Non-regression of this
static model can still stall or reject a useful maneuver; complete adaptive games
must decide whether the strategy helps.

Deliver owned validated work and evidence to fresh `origin/main`, preserve
unrelated changes, and leave the checkout on `main`. A failed or inconclusive
experiment leaves the full competitive goal active.

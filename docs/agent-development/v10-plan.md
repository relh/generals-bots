The goal remains a winning advantage against every available distinct opponent.
It is not achieved. The [selected V10 experiment](v10-development.md) rescues
one consumed my_bot9 position in both mirrored complete games. This plan tests
whether that narrow improvement survives broader opposition and preserves
stronger controls. It does not test a donation-accounting repair.

Freeze the delivered V10 policy
`7095167e547af5edbf55b528939e4fc7537c954b97ad7370c258ce8090209b2c`
and its complete current source closure before new matches. Keep four fixed arms:
V6, V9, V10 with the V6 parent (`v10-v6`), and V10 with the V9 parent (`v10`).
The two enabled candidates use the identical home-mobilization rule; parent
selection is fixed before JIT compilation. Their disabled modes already have
exact action, memory and telemetry parity tests. No policy tuning or candidate
selection is allowed during this experiment. V2 remains the default.

## Decision criteria

Report every arm's W/L/D and score separately for each opponent. Primary
comparisons are V10-over-V6 minus V6 and V10-over-V9 minus V9. Also report both
cross-parent comparisons, giving 24 fixed contrasts. Use 100,000 whole-map
resamples with NumPy default_rng seed 19983 reset per contrast. Four or eight
map clusters support each interval; mirrors are not independent observations.
Intervals are descriptive and unadjusted for multiple comparisons.

A useful parent-relative change must preserve the parent's development score
against every opponent, improve a relevant complete-game outcome, and have
zero candidate invalid/malformed actions with valid runtime. Report each
parent-win-to-draw/loss regression even if other games compensate. Advancing
toward the overall goal additionally requires no score regression against the
stronger of V6 and V9 for any opponent and all existing local/deployment gates.
Passing this consumed-map gate is insufficient for promotion: the separate
fresh-map per-opponent score interval lower bound must exceed 50%.

Audit complete changed trajectories after the global budget, separating parent
proposal telemetry from issued actions and first-transfer coverage from later
route safety. Ordinary-combat failures before turn 800, including the known
Juraj V3.5 collapse, are specificity checks and remaining gaps; do not attribute
their repair to this touch-only rule without an actual changed trajectory.

Continue the opponent census separately. Six measured implementations do not
cover every public entrant or every family variant. Report unavailable policies
and adapter gaps explicitly. Deliver owned validated work to fresh `origin/main`
after collision checks and leave the primary checkout on `main`. A completed
experiment does not close the competitive goal.

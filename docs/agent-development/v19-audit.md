# Before V19: delivery and defender release

V18's completed screen remains rejected: two wins and four losses. These
public-state audits motivate [V19's bounded retention experiment](v19-plan.md);
they establish no counterfactual rescue or new strength result. No inference or
new games were used in these audits.

## Late force is not necessarily deliverable

Across 32 original V18 and winning V10 Amin observations, exhaustive simple
owned paths of at most twelve moves expose concrete reinforcement options.
At V18 turn 532 a twelve-move path adds 39 armies, reaching home with 60 under
uncontested growth; eleven moves add 32, reaching 52. At 534 the best ten-move
path adds 28; at 545 one move adds only one. Independent mutable-board arithmetic
checks all 204 selected path/budget results. These maxima apply only to the
specified static simple-path model. The largest visible attacker's Manhattan
distance is not a safe delivery deadline: smaller hostile surplus is already
four steps away at 532, and interception, growth and move ordering matter.

A separate exact knapsack on one deterministic shortest-owned BFS tree allows
branch collection within twelve edges. At 532 it adds 34 armies; at 534 it also
adds 34. Stationary route deficits remain 34 and 23 respectively after collection
and uncontested home growth. The tree restricts routes and is not a global
optimum; its twelve-action budget can exceed the remaining attack deadline.
Neither audit demonstrates sufficient late defense. The winning V10 control
also has smaller single-path options in several sampled states, so this metric
alone does not distinguish a losing strategy.

## The inherited alarm can disappear while force remains visible

Independent reconstruction of 23 Amin/Juraj observations matches all 17 recorded
selected-threat deficits and feasibility results. At Amin 535 the visible army
95 is eleven terrain steps from home, with stationary deficit 48. The inherited
planner's horizon is ten, so it returns default threat and deficit fields. The
same force was within the gate at 534 and returns at 536. A zero telemetry
deficit therefore does not certify coverage.

The interceptor requires targets to lie on both a cheapest-attrition corridor
and a geometrically shortest route. That excludes some timely partial transfers,
but no sufficient excluded route was established. Even before target sampling,
the best timely allowed reinforcement at 534/536 contributes 28 against required
53/43. Merely enlarging the horizon or changing target sampling has no proven
rescue here. Existing late army elsewhere is not equivalent to available defense.

## A real defender completes its target, then leaves

At Juraj 438 V18 starts an actual defender toward cell 129, with expected arrival
18 and original expiry 448. Visible need disappears at 439. The original action
still reaches the target, but native commitment clears. The next two actions
reverse the packet. Leaving at 440 removes 17 points of stationary public route
resistance. By 442 no one-step move from the actual returned position closes the
21-point deficit. Fog prevents identifying later sightings as the same attacker;
these observations do not establish that retaining the defender would win.

The stronger winning control supplies important counterexamples to unconditional
retention. At 477 its released home-bound packet later contributes to construction;
a visible force lies outside the ten-step horizon. At 805 an expired defender
redeploys and later builds. At 923 another expired defender physically screens
for many turns despite cleared memory. At 947 a guard releases a packet that
captures an enemy castle at 948. This last action is an ordinary castle capture,
not a general/deathtouch capture; a half move fails and leaves six enemy armies.
All six original V10/V10-v6/V18 Juraj histories and their mirrors were audited,
with 41 witness frames and nine release events. Independent weighted-distance
methods agree. Two superseded arithmetic reports are retained with their
corrections, including the castle classification and failed-half remainder.

V19 therefore tests continued use of an already committed defender under its
original expiry, with actual build and nonowned-move priorities preserved. It
adds no remembered enemy force and no permanent reserve. Retention can still
suppress productive redeployment; the complete stronger-control gate must decide
whether the tradeoff works.

The [artifact manifest](v19-audit-evidence.json) binds the audits, original input
hashes, arithmetic checks and retained attempts. V2 remains the default and the
competitive objective remains unmet.
